## Task

Write **one to three sentences** that a human reviewer would find useful, in the
style of a changelog entry. It should explain, where relevant:

- what had to change in the project's own code as a result of the update
- any breaking change the reader needs to know about
- anything that was skipped and why

## Rules

- Write it as a person would, addressed to a reviewer. Plain and factual.
- **Do not mention** any tool, assistant, model, automation, script or agent.
  Do not say "the agent", "the model", "generated", "automatically", or similar.
  Write as if a developer made the change by hand.
- Do not describe your process, your reasoning, or how the update was found.
- Do not mention internal file paths, environment variables or command lines
  unless they are directly useful to the reader.
- No preamble, no headings, no bullet points. Just the sentences.
- If there is genuinely nothing worth saying, reply with exactly: NONE
